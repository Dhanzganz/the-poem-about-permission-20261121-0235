Entry for the make.fun bounty **the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235** ("The poem about permission").

The program writes one short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given. It is written as the voice of the one who asks: a key is handed over, a room is entered, and the giver can take it all back with a single word. The turn is the poem's insight — the giving is the power, and the asking is too — so agency is not a thing one side owns and the other lacks, but a bargain both sides keep.

## The poem

> I asked, and you handed me the key —
> not the door, not the house, just the key.
> I turned it. The room was mine to enter,
> yours to take back with a word.
>
> That is the bargain we keep, you and I:
> I ask, you give, and the giving
> is the power. So is the asking.

## How it works

- `main.py` — Python 3.13, standard library only, no network. Writes the fixed poem deterministically to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, or `/output/entry.txt` when the variable is unset. The text is 8 lines, under 280 characters, identical bytes on every run.
